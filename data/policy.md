# Northwind Analytics — Travel and Expense Policy (synthetic)

Version 2026.2. Clause ids (for example "4.2" or "Appendix B") are the citation keys.
All amounts are in US dollars.

# 1 General

## 1.1 Purpose
This policy covers business travel and expenses paid by employees and reimbursed through the
expense system. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary and for company business.

# 2 Booking and categories

## 2.1 Booking channel
Book flights, hotels and rental cars through the company travel tool. Bookings made outside the
travel tool need a short reason in the expense memo.

## 2.2 Expense categories
Code each expense line to the category that matches what was bought: Meals, Lodging, Airfare,
Ground Transportation, Mileage or Other. A hotel stay is Lodging even when it is paid at a
restaurant or bar inside the hotel; a meal is Meals even when it is charged to the room.

# 3 Receipts and submission

## 3.1 Receipts
A receipt is required for any line over $75. Lines of $75 or less do not need a receipt.

## 3.2 Submission deadline
Submit expense reports within 60 days of the transaction date. Late reports need the
approval of the employee's manager.

## 3.5 Duplicate submissions
Duplicate submissions are not reimbursable. An expense that was already submitted on an earlier
line or report must not be claimed again.

# 4 Meals

## 4.1 Meals
Meals are reimbursed up to the per-person limits in Appendix B for the city tier.

## 4.2 Dinner
Dinner: per-person limit by city tier, see Appendix B; amendments in Appendix C apply.

## 4.3 Client entertainment
Client entertainment: up to $150 per person, alcohol included. Attendee names and company are
required (see 4.4).

## 4.4 Attendees
Every group meal lists the number of attendees. Client meals list the name and company of every
attendee.

## 4.5 Alcohol
Alcohol is not reimbursable, except as part of client entertainment under 4.3.

# 5 Lodging

## 5.1 Lodging
Lodging: per-night limit by city tier, see Appendix B.

## 5.2 Hotel booking
Book a standard room. Room upgrades paid by the employee are not reimbursable.

# 6 Transportation

## 6.1 Airfare
Airfare: economy. Business class only when the scheduled flight time is over 6 hours.

## 6.2 Baggage
Fees for up to two checked bags per trip are reimbursable.

## 6.3 Ground transportation
Taxis, rideshare, airport shuttles and public transit to and from business locations are
reimbursable.

## 6.4 Mileage
Use of a personal car is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Do not claim fuel separately.

# 7 Personal items and tips

## 7.1 Personal items
Personal items are not reimbursable: minibar, in-room movies, spa, personal entertainment.

## 7.2 Tips
Reasonable tips for meals, taxis and hotel staff are reimbursable, up to 20% of the bill.

# 8 Approvals and recovery

## 8.1 Approvals
Out of policy under $250 -> the employee's manager. $250 or more, any non-reimbursable alcohol
(4.5) or personal item, or a DIRECTOR's own line -> Finance. Alcohol allowed as part of client
entertainment under 4.3 does not by itself send a line to Finance.

## 8.2 Recovery
Not yet paid -> reject the line before payment. Already paid -> recover from the employee by
payroll deduction (Finance).

# Appendices

## Appendix B City tiers and limits
Tier 1 cities: San Francisco, New York, London, Boston, Seattle. All other cities are Tier 2.
Tier 1 dinner, per person: $100
Tier 2 dinner, per person: $70
Other meals (breakfast, lunch), per person: $40
Tier 1 lodging, per night: $300
Tier 2 lodging, per night: $200

## Appendix C Amendments
C-1 (effective 2026-10-01): the Tier 1 dinner limit is $85 per person. This amendment replaces
the Tier 1 dinner amount in Appendix B for dinners on or after 2026-10-01. Tier 2 limits are not
changed.
